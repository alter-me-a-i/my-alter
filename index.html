how it fits together

Three pieces, working only for you

Think of it as three small parts on your own computer. Each one has a simple job, and none of them phone home.

1

The extension in your browser

A lightweight browser add-on that captures and filters only what you choose to keep. You decide what it pays attention to — it ignores everything else.

2

The Vault app on your computer

A private, encrypted store that lives on your own machine. Your data is locked, and you hold the key — a master password only you know. Nothing is stored in the cloud.

3

The cortex the engine in the middle

The quiet engine that connects the two — and acts as a gatekeeper. It decides what's allowed to reach your Vault. Everything happens on your machine; nothing leaves it.

The flow is simple: you choose → the extension captures → the cortex checks it → your Vault keeps it, encrypted. Every step stays on your computer.

set it up

Get your vault running

Five plain steps. Some parts are still early, so where there isn't a one-click install yet, we'll say so honestly.

1

Get the extension coming soon

This is the browser add-on that captures what you choose. It isn't in the browser stores yet, so for now you add it from the source. Grab it here: github.com/alter-me-a-i/alter-me ↗.

2

Get the Vault app coming soon

This is your private, encrypted store. A packaged download is on the way; for now you run it from the source. Get it here: github.com/alter-me-a-i/vault ↗, and follow its setup notes.

3

Open your Vault

Start the Vault app and set your master password. That password is your key — it never leaves your machine, and only you can open the Vault with it. Keep it somewhere safe; nobody can recover it for you.

4

Connect them

Your Vault app has an access token — a short code that lets the extension talk to it. Copy that code, open the extension's Vault section, and paste it into the “Vault access token” box. Click “Save & test” to confirm they're linked.

5

Start capturing

Turn on “Send to Vault” in the extension. From now on, whatever you choose to capture flows straight into your private vault — encrypted, on your computer, yours alone.

One thing to remember: your Vault lives only on this computer. Nothing you capture ever leaves your machine — there's no account to sign up for and no server holding your data. Want the deeper details? The Vault docs and the GitHub org have everything.

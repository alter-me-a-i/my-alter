alter / me / a / i

Privacy Policy

Effective June 10, 2026
The short version: alter/me/a/i is local-first. Everything it captures is stored in an encrypted vault on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, sent to us, shared, or sold. There are no servers, no analytics, no ads, no third-party tracking.

What the extension does

alter/me/a/i keeps a private, on-device copy of your own web activity — the same data that cookies and trackers collect about you — and blocks third-party tracking and browser fingerprinting as you browse. You own and control all of it.

What it accesses

  • Web history — page URLs, titles, and times of visit.
  • Website content — page text and the text you select, copied into your local vault.
  • User activity — search queries and text from non-password forms you submit.

It explicitly excludes password fields — anything inside a form that contains a password is never captured.

Where your data lives

In a local, encrypted vault on your own device. Only you hold the key — it is derived from your passphrase, which we never see and which has no recovery. No copy of your captured data is ever sent to us or to any external server.

What we never do

  • No servers. We operate none, and your data never leaves your machine.
  • No selling or transferring your data to third parties.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no behavioral profiling by us.
  • No use of your data for creditworthiness or lending.

The one optional connection

You may choose to connect the extension to your own Vault companion app running on your own computer, over a local loopback address (127.0.0.1:8787). This stays entirely on your device, is off by default, and contacts no remote server.

Your controls

  • Turn capture on or off at any time.
  • Choose how much protection to apply (the protection personas).
  • Lock the vault, and set it to auto-lock after a timeout.
  • Erase everything with one action — irreversibly, because only you hold the key.

Permissions, briefly

  • storage — saves your settings and the vault's salt, on your device.
  • declarativeNetRequest — blocks third-party trackers and strips third-party cookies/referrers.
  • scripting — applies your chosen anti-fingerprinting protections in the page.
  • alarms — auto-locks the vault after your chosen timeout.
  • host access — lets capture and protection run on the sites you visit. Captured data stays local.

Children

alter/me/a/i is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

alter/me/a/i is free and open source. Questions or concerns: open an issue at github.com/alter-me-a-i.
